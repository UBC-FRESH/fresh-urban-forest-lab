Label definitions
  • Conifer: a conifer tree group under the crosshair: needles/scale foliage, conical or layered crown form, or clearly identifiable conifer habit.
  • Deciduous: a broadleaf/deciduous tree group under the crosshair, including flowering or bare-crown deciduous trees.
  • Uncertain: the crop is too ambiguous, the crosshair is on an edge, or you cannot tell tree from building/vegetation. Do not guess.

Mixed canopy: label by the red X. If the crosshair is on a conifer, choose Conifer even if deciduous branches overlap; note “mixed canopy”. If the crosshair is on a deciduous crown, choose Deciduous. If the point is between crowns or obscured, choose Uncertain.

Mask overreach: label by the red X. If the X is on a tree but the orange mask spills onto sidewalk, road, cars, roofs, or lawn, choose the group of the tree under the X and flag mask overreach. The candidate is still a tree, but the crown polygon is not usable for area or volume. If the X itself is on the sidewalk/car/road, choose Uncertain.

The red X is the candidate point, not a claim that a tree is there. If the X falls on the edge of a clearly identifiable tree crown, label Conifer and note “edge”. If it falls outside the crown, label Deciduous or Uncertain. Click the image to open the full crop. Labels save to the review server; browser storage is a fallback. Export often. This is a visual pre-audit, not a field measurement.
