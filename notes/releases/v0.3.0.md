## What this is

The integrated FRESH Urban Forest Lab: a public, reproducible urban-forest modelling and
decision-support prototype. It links public LiDAR, tree inventories, individual-tree
growth and water, observed change, Fresh Solutions optimization, off-inventory canopy, and
bird habitat/demography in one local web application.

## Headline capabilities

- **Shade and species:** marginal time-integrated shade, building/canopy competition,
  species fit, water balance, growth/vigour, and irrigation cost.
- **Optimization:** Fresh Solutions v0.8.0 scalarizations, parallel workers, fuzzy Pareto
  archive, 3D solution-space explorer, saved archives, pin/compare, and load-as-scenario.
- **Off-inventory canopy:** unmatched LiDAR crown candidates with confidence and land-class
  proxy; value-of-information comparison.
- **Birds:** 3D habitat metrics, five guilds, relative-abundance index, prior-driven
  two-stage demography, lambda with prior range and survival curve.
- **Observed change:** UBC 2015/2016/2021 and City of Vancouver 2018/2022 products.
- **Unified report:** one JSON bundle across metrics, shade, birds, completeness,
  off-inventory, evidence, and caveats.
- **Methods and ground classification:** clean-room point-cloud primitives (P12), ALS
  ground classification and validation (P13-P14), and a manually checked ground patch plus
  an optional FEMIC sidecar adapter design (P15).

## Evidence highlights

- UBC core demo: 66.6% canopy cover, 4,861 live trees, 2.0k t above-ground carbon.
- UBC 2021 full processed dataset: 9,324 tree records; 9,259 live.
- Observed UBC 2016→2021 same-mask: net +123,182 m² canopy, mean height +0.420 m.
- Whole-campus optimizer: 387 sites, 72 scalarizations, 1,010,952 evaluations, 71 archive
  solutions, hypervolume 0.0508.
- Z* convergence: patience stop after 36 rounds, 112,116 evaluations, best balanced
  objective -0.293285.
- Off-inventory Main Mall pilot: 5,536 detected, 1,409 matched, 500 candidates, 8.3 ha.
- Bird baseline: habitat score 0.78; guild lambda range 0.78–1.09 (prior).

## Known gaps

- No field-validated shade, bird, off-inventory, or growth dataset.
- No local mark-recapture survival estimate.
- No parcel/ownership layer for off-inventory trees.
- City LiDAR coverage is two core tiles, not the whole city.
- Weighted-sum optimization is a finite Pareto approximation.
- Dose-response and demographic priors are literature/expert priors.
