# v0.6.0

Status: released. Tag: `v0.6.0`.
Date: 2026-11-14.

## What this is

The Walk mode v2 release. It keeps the v0.5.0 data products and scientific defaults and
adds street-level navigation to the 3D scene.

## What changed

- Added **Street** spawn at the nearest OSM road centreline, facing along the street.
- Added building-footprint collision with spatial indexing and axis sliding.
- Added a 0.72 m step-up limit and smoothed terrain-following eye height.
- Added compass, UTM coordinates, ground elevation, and a local minimap.
- Shadows are disabled during Walk and restored on Orbit exit.
- Preserved pointer lock, WASD/arrows, Shift sprint, Escape, and Orbit behavior.
- Added Walk v2 controls and limits to the web application guide.

## Not changed

- No scientific product, data claim, default layer, or corrected-product threshold changed.
- Corrected change/action/turnover layers remain the UI defaults; original lineages remain
  selectable.
- P60 retains the constant **+0.011 m** vertical correction and the western-outlier caveat.

## Limits

- Building footprints block movement; interiors, stairs, doors, and full physics are not
  modelled.
- Trees are not collision objects.
- Walk mode is in-engine navigation, not Google Street View imagery.
- Headless software rendering was slow at full-city street level. A hardware-GPU
  frame-rate benchmark is still required.
- Four western vertical-control outliers and the P32 review gap remain documented.
