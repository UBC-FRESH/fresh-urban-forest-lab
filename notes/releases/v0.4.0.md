Status: release candidate; tag pending explicit approval.
Date: 2026-11-09.

## What this is

The corrected full-city urban-forest release. It connects public Vancouver LiDAR and tree
inventory data to canopy metrics, shade/equity and gap screens, observed change, action and
turnover products, 3D optimization, site scenarios, and full-control corrected products.

## Headline capabilities

- **Full Vancouver pilot:** 2022 LiDAR, 184,152 public-tree records, local-area metrics,
  city-scale 2D/3D views.
- **Shade and equity:** P38 shade/social-equity screen and P40 Landsat LST heat-equity
  screen.
- **Canopy gaps:** P39 above-ground gap/opportunity screen.
- **Observed change:** P41 downtown and P43 citywide 2018→2022 2 m canopy-change screens.
- **Action and turnover:** P42/P44 integrated action screen and P46 turnover hotspots.
- **Optimization:** P47 hover/ranges/parallel coordinates/brushes/URL/CSV, P48 site
  scenarios, P49 scenario comparison and presets.
- **Vertical control:** P50/P51/P53 full-city ground control, corrected P43, rebuilt P38,
  and corrected P44/P46 sidecars. P54 publishes corrected versions as UI defaults while
  keeping original lineage selectable. P55 documents western outlier limitations.
- **Portal:** UBC, Vancouver, Surrey, Burnaby, and Coquitlam demos.

## Corrected product

- Full control sample: 181/181 tiles, 149 usable control tiles, 965,995 class-2 pairs.
- Median control offset: **+0.011 m**.
- P43 correction: **+0.989 m**.
- Corrected P43: **−94.29 ha** net, **+0.519 m** stable height.
- Original P43: **+223 ha**, **+1.475 m**.
- Corrected action/turnover are default in the UI; original versions remain selectable.

## Known gaps

- Four western control outliers are documented; no independent roof/road/external control
  available.
- No field validation or survey-grade accuracy claim.
- Corrected screen is not a final citywide net-loss claim.
- P32 still needs a second human reviewer; P37 remains separate.
- No ownership, survival, planting, or causal claim.
