# v0.8.0

Status: released. Tag: `v0.8.0`.
Date: 2026-11-16.

## What this is

The street-tree audit checkpoint. It keeps the v0.7.0 scientific products and publishes the
full live-host KartaView screening layer, its demo count/caveat, and the P68/P69 uncertainty
audits.

## What changed

- P67 processed all **6,425** live-host KartaView images reachable through the 118-cell,
  100-record scan and published **19,790** fused street-tree candidates.
- 6,078 candidates match public inventory within 8 m; median matched distance is **4.99 m**.
- The Vancouver demo shows a dynamic `street trees · 19,790` count and a screening caveat.
- P68 audited FOV/heading sensitivity and inventory-match tolerance.
- P69 audited 200 detector boxes:
  - median tree-mask fraction **0.703**;
  - 42/200 boxes below 50% tree pixels;
  - 87/200 bottom-centre pixels labelled `tree`.
- A Grounding DINO `tree trunk` trial was descriptive only and was not integrated.
- Preserved the P66 1,167-image sample, the 24-candidate Commercial Drive pilot, and the
  85-image sparse sample separately.
- Added P68/P69 operator audit scripts, reports, regression tests, and documentation.

## Not changed

- Street candidates remain a separate `street_level_cv` layer.
- No merge with LiDAR off-inventory, official inventory, or corrected products.
- No new detector or camera calibration was released.
- The canonical layer remains a screening product; the external multi-view subset is not
  served by the demo.
- Scientific products, default layers, and Walk mode are unchanged.

## Limits

- Full live-host KartaView set reachable through the bounded scan, not a complete street
  census or every historical record.
- FOV remains assumed at 60°; camera pitch is unmodeled.
- Imagery is predominantly 2016-2018; inventory is current.
- Detector boxes often include canopy, sky, vehicles, facades, or signs; the bottom-centre
  depth is not necessarily a trunk.
- No field validation, precision/recall, ownership, survival, private-tree census, or
  complete-census claim.
